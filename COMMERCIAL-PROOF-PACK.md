## TARGET 1 — Dr.Delagen

Market: DTC skincare, anti-aging, primarily women 45+ in US/AU/CA.
Public demand signal: active/recent hiring for a Creative Strategist whose mission is Research → Insight → Angle → Concept → Brief → Production → Testing → Analysis → Iteration.
Public success target: 10 concepts/week, typically ~3 hook variations each.
Publicly stated business context: over US$4M revenue through Meta Ads in the previous 12 months and an aggressive Q4 growth target.

### Spec hypothesis A — "The overnight ritual"
Audience tension: generic skincare routines feel complicated and overloaded.
Hook family:
1. "What if your night routine ended with one satisfying step?"
2. "The skincare step you actually look forward to peeling off."
3. "Your night routine does not need twelve products."
Creative path: creator-native routine → application texture → morning/peel ritual → product detail → CTA.
Compliance: do not invent efficacy. Use only substantiated, approved claims supplied by the brand.

### Spec hypothesis B — "Made for 45+, not borrowed from 25+"
Audience tension: women 45+ are often shown generic beauty messaging that does not speak directly to them.
Hook family:
1. "Skincare for 45+ should not feel like an afterthought."
2. "Your skin changed. Your creative should too."
3. "Stop talking to mature skin like it is twenty-five."
Creative path: direct-to-camera creator → specific mature-skin routine objections → product usage → proof supplied by brand → CTA.
Test variable: identity/age-specific framing versus generic anti-aging framing.

### Spec hypothesis C — "Mechanism before miracle"
Audience tension: skepticism toward overpromising skincare ads.
Hook family:
1. "No miracle language. Show me what the mask actually does."
2. "Before the claim, show the mechanism."
3. "The fastest way to lose trust? Promise too much."
Creative path: product macro → texture/application → explain approved mechanism in simple language → customer proof if verified → CTA.
Test variable: evidence-first framing versus aspirational framing.

Why this target fits Creative Ops:
- explicitly wants hypothesis-driven creative
- explicitly wants winner/loser learning loops
- explicitly wants AI used as workflow acceleration, not generic prompting
- already has editors/designers, so our zero-cash strategy layer can be sold before video automation
