## Strongest scaling wedge
### Agency / portfolio distribution
A brand sale yields one tenant.
An agency sale can yield multiple client accounts.

Evidence:
- agencies are hiring strategists across 3–5 or more accounts;
- one current agency listing says ~50 active clients;
- another listing requires 8–12 briefs/day across 4–5 brands;
- multi-shop operators explicitly need several stores handled in parallel.

Therefore the platform should support:
AGENCY → CLIENT TENANTS → PRODUCTS → CHANNELS → CREATIVE JOBS → PERFORMANCE → LEARNING

White-label / agency mode is a high-priority future capability.

## Biggest commercial blocker
We have no portfolio with attributable performance.

Many high-value buyer listings explicitly ask for:
- 2–3+ years experience
- examples that spent real ad budget
- CTR/CPA/ROAS/hook-rate evidence
- proof that the strategist personally influenced results

This is the main reason not to attack only the biggest US$3k–8k roles first.

## Entry strategy for a new company
Do not pretend to have results we do not have.
Attack with:
- outside-in private spec
- one unusually strong creative hypothesis
- finished storyboard / production-ready brief
- optional founder-funded proof asset under a strict budget
- transparent statement that private performance analysis starts after account data is provided

Initial buyer selection should favor:
- explicit willingness to test
- clear product and public evidence
- ongoing/contract-to-hire opportunity
- lower portfolio barrier
- ability to show value before account access
