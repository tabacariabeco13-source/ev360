# CREATIVE WASTE AUDIT — COMMERCIAL SPEC v0.1
Date: 2026-10-04
Status: TEST OFFER, NOT YET MARKET-PROVEN

## Positioning
We audit where creative-testing spend is failing to produce useful commercial learning, and convert that into a prioritized next-test plan.

We do NOT claim:
- a fixed percentage of waste,
- guaranteed ROAS,
- causal certainty without a controlled test,
- recoverable cash unless a buyer's data supports that conclusion.

## Why this offer exists
Fresh buyer evidence shows demand for:
- paid audit first,
- creative diagnosis,
- identifying fatigue / wasted spend,
- 30-day test plans,
- explaining what is happening, why, and what to do next,
- budget allocation tied to profitability,
- documented learnings feeding the next round.

## Core output
1. Creative inventory.
2. What was actually tested.
3. Variables changed vs mixed.
4. Tests that generated interpretable learning.
5. Tests that were inconclusive.
6. Repeated tests / duplicated concepts.
7. Suspected fatigue vs non-fatigue deterioration.
8. Spend on inconclusive / duplicate / structurally invalid tests.
9. Five prioritized next tests.
10. Measurement rules for each next test.
11. Explicit confidence level and data gaps.

## Waste taxonomy
Never call all underperformance "waste".

Possible waste / leakage classes:
- DUPLICATE_TEST: repeated concept without a new hypothesis.
- MIXED_VARIABLES: too many variables changed to attribute the result.
- INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE: stopped before declared minimum signal.
- NO_DECISION_LINK: result exists but did not alter the next decision.
- FATIGUE_MISDIAGNOSIS: creative replaced without enough evidence fatigue was the cause.
- WRONG_METRIC: optimization judged on a metric disconnected from business objective.
- UNCONTROLLED_TEST: comparison invalid because important variables were not held stable.
- PRODUCTION_MISMATCH: delivered asset did not match the brief/hypothesis.
- ECONOMICALLY_INVALID: test cannot be profitable at the current unit economics even if media metric looks healthy.

## Conservative value calculation
Report three layers separately:

A. OBSERVED NON-INFORMATIVE SPEND
Spend tied to tests that clearly meet an auditable leakage rule.

B. ESTIMATED AVOIDABLE REWORK
Production / media cost that could plausibly have been avoided, labeled ESTIMATE.

C. OPPORTUNITY COST
Do not monetize by default. Report qualitatively unless the buyer supplies a defensible model.

Never add A+B+C into one "money recovered" number without explicit assumptions.

The client receives:
- evidence-backed waste/leakage findings,
- confidence per finding,
- exact data reference,
- five prioritized tests,
- explicit next action.

## Commercial proof required
This offer becomes VALIDATED only after:
1. at least 1 paid audit,
2. delivery cost/time measured,
3. buyer confirms at least one finding is actionable,
4. buyer executes at least one recommendation OR requests continuation,
5. unit economics are positive.

Three paid audits with repeat/continuation begin to prove repeatability.
