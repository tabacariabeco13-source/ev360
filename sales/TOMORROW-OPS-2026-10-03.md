## What we do for each reply

### POSITIVE
Do not celebrate first. Qualify scope.
Ask for the minimum private inputs:
- offer / landing page
- approved + prohibited claims
- 3 winners / 3 losers if available
- success metric
- raw assets only if production is included

Then:
scope → quote guard → delivery window → payment step → start.

### PRICING
Never answer from instinct.
Run:
price → provider cost → infra → fees → our hours → contingency → margin.
If margin misses the floor:
reprice or reduce scope.

### SPEC_REQUEST
Send the private spec.
Do not send a complete free implementation.
End with one narrow paid next step.

### CALL_REQUEST
Prepare:
- 3 buyer-specific observations
- 1 non-obvious hypothesis
- 1 proposed pilot
- 1 price band only after quote guard
- 1 clear boundary around what is not yet automated

### NOT_NOW
Record reason and next sensible follow-up date.

### DECLINE
Capture why:
budget / timing / proof / scope / trust / no need / wrong person.
This becomes market learning.

### DO_NOT_CONTACT
Suppress future outreach immediately.
